Who's behind the lens

Ryan Trotti

Hey y'all

My name is Ryan Trotti, and I am a second-year sports business major at the University of Samford in Birmingham, Alabama.

Ever since I was young, I have been fascinated by sports. Growing up, I played everything from soccer to lacrosse to baseball, and I swam competitively too. In high school, I ended up sticking to basketball and lacrosse as my main two sports.

While playing, I always wanted to have cool pictures to show my family and friends, but every photographer was too overpriced. That's why I decided to start my own photography project that I call RShots.

I offer affordable pricing to kids and families who want to have high-quality event pictures at a price that doesn't burn a hole in their pocket.
